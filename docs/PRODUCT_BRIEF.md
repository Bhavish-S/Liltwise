# Product brief

## Product

**Liltwise — Your AI Co-Presenter**  
**Tagline:** Stay present. Never lose your words.

Liltwise is a cross-platform product for helping people prepare and deliver talks in online meetings, webinars, presentations, conferences, and live events.

## People it is for

- Professionals presenting updates, demos, or proposals.
- Educators and trainers delivering prepared material.
- Speakers preparing for webinars, conferences, and live events.
- Anyone who wants a prompt for their next thought without losing connection with the audience.

## Problems to solve

- Preparing a script from an event guide and audience context takes time.
- Reading notes can make a speaker lose eye contact or lose their place.
- A pause, skipped paragraph, or interruption can make it difficult to recover smoothly.
- Speakers need a practical way to keep notes visible while they present.

## Intended capabilities

1. A website for accounts, event preparation, event guides, speech drafting and editing, saved scripts, and desktop downloads.
2. A macOS and Windows companion with a discreet floating teleprompter where supported by the operating system.
3. A future adaptive speaking engine that can follow speech and help a speaker recover their place.
4. Future AI assistance for event context, recovery prompts, timing, and supported meeting integrations.

## Product principles

- The core script-reading and recovery experience must not depend on a particular meeting provider.
- Ask permission before using a microphone, and do not retain audio by default.
- Be honest about operating-system and screen-sharing limitations.
- Keep private credentials and AI provider keys on secure server-side systems.
- Build the core macOS and Windows experience before considering Linux.

## Current milestone

Milestone 1 creates a responsive marketing website and a secure Electron welcome shell. It does not implement authentication, event storage, speech generation, speech tracking, microphone capture, or the floating teleprompter.
